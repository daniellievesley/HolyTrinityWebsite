Welcome to Holy Trinity Church, Frinton-on-Sea.

Whether you’ve come across us through a service, been invited by someone, or are exploring faith, we’re really glad you’re here.

Most importantly, we want you to know the Good News of Jesus Christ: that whatever you are facing, God is near, and He offers hope, peace, and new life through Him.

If life feels heavy or uncertain, please feel free to contact us — we would love to listen, pray with you, and help you find encouragement in Jesus.

All are warmly welcome.
